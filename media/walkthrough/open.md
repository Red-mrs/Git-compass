# Your Git cockpit

Click the **compass icon** in the activity bar (the vertical bar on the far left).

- **Overview** – current branch, push/pull status, a suggestion for the next step, and the **commit box**.
- **Changes** – what you changed. Click a file to see the difference.
- **Commits / Branches / Stashes / Tags** – your history and lines of work.
- **File History / Compare / Undo History** – explore and recover.
- **Pull Requests** – GitHub reviews.

Hover anything for an explanation. Right-click for more actions.
